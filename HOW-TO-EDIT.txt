BIRTHDAY WEBSITE - HOW TO MAKE IT YOURS
=======================================

1. YOUR VIDEOS
   Put your videos in the "videos" folder and name them
   video1.mp4, video2.mp4, video3.mp4, video4.mp4, video5.mp4
   (replace the sample files). Any size or shape works: phone videos,
   wide videos and square ones are all fitted automatically.

   Use .mp4 files. iPhone videos saved as .mov (HEVC) may not play in
   Chrome or on Android, so export or convert them to .mp4 (H.264) first.

2. YOUR PHOTOS
   Put your photos in the "photos" folder:
   hero.jpg     the photo on the first screen
   photo1.jpg ... photo6.jpg   the photo section
   Any size or shape works here too.

3. NAME, DATE, CAPTIONS AND THE BIG WISH
   Open index.html in any text editor (Notepad, TextEdit, VS Code)
   and search for "EDIT HERE". Change:
   name, birthday (YYYY-MM-DD), captions, and the finale message.

   Want more or fewer videos/photos? Add or remove lines in the
   "videos" and "photos" lists. Different file names are fine as long
   as the list matches.

   Don't want the cake section? Set   showCake: false

4. VIEW IT
   Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

5. SHARE IT
   Upload the whole folder (index.html + videos + photos) to any free
   static host, for example Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or Vercel, and
   send the link. Keep large videos under about 50 MB each so the page
   stays fast on phones.
